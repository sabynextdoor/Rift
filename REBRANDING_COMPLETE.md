## 🎯 Design Decisions

### Why "Rift" instead of "RIFT"?
- **More approachable**: Title case feels less corporate
- **Better readability**: Easier to read in various contexts
- **Modern branding**: Follows contemporary SaaS naming trends
- **Flexibility**: Works better in different sizes and contexts

### Why "by Saby"?
- **Creator attribution**: Clear ownership and credit
- **Personal brand**: Builds Saby's portfolio
- **Trust signal**: Shows real person behind the product
- **Differentiation**: Unique branding in the market

### Two-Line Logo Treatment
```
Rift
by Saby
```

**Benefits:**
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Professional appearance
- Maintains brand recognition
- Credits creator prominently
- Works at all sizes
